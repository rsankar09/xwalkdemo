Key takeaways

  • An IRA is a tax-advantaged retirement account that can help you build retirement savings.
  • Traditional and Roth IRAs offer different tax benefits.
  • IRAs have annual contribution limits and withdrawal rules.
Traditional IRA Roth IRA
Money in No taxes on contributions or growth Contributions are after-tax, but growth is not taxed
Money out Taxed as income on withdrawal No taxes on qualified withdrawals in retirement
How much does it cost to start an IRA?

There is no minimum amount required by law to open an IRA, though some providers set their own minimums.

What is the best age to cash out an IRA?

Withdrawals are generally penalty free from age 59½.

What is the 5-year rule for IRAs?

Roth earnings must stay in the account five years before they can be withdrawn tax free.
